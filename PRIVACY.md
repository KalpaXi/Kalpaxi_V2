# Privacy notice

KalpaXi is free, open-source software. This notice explains what personal data
KalpaXi collects, why, and your rights, in line with India's
**Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** and its Rules.

## What we collect, and when

Only when you **register during installation**:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Name | To address you in emails |
| Email address | To send the one-time installation code, and to inform you about upgraded functionality |
| Profession (Student, Academic, Design & Consultancy Firm, Small and Medium Enterprise, Enthusiast) | To understand who uses KalpaXi and improve it for them |
| Installation date and time, KalpaXi version | To know which versions are in use |
| A random installation ID | To recognise the same computer if you reinstall (it is not your hardware serial number or MAC address) |
| IP address of the registration request | Recorded by the server for security and abuse prevention |

## What we do **not** collect

- Your CAD models, drawings or analysis results. **They stay on your computer**; analysis runs locally.
- Usage tracking, analytics or crash data from inside the software.

## Purpose

1. Confirm your email address during installation.
2. Inform you **only when upgraded functionality is added** to KalpaXi. No advertising and no offers from others.
3. Understand our users and use your inputs and feedback to improve KalpaXi.

We use your data on the basis of the **consent** you give in the installer.

## Who processes your data

- **Email delivery:** our email service provider (Brevo) sends the installation code and update emails.
- **Hosting:** the registration server runs with our hosting provider.

These providers act only on our instructions. We **do not sell or share** your data with anyone else.

## How long we keep it

As long as you remain a registered user, or until you ask us to delete it.
If the KalpaXi project stops, the data will be deleted.

## Your rights

You can, at any time, by writing to **admin@kalpaxi.in**:

- ask what data we hold about you
- ask us to correct or update it
- ask us to delete it
- withdraw your consent, including stopping update emails
- raise a complaint (grievance)
- nominate another person to exercise these rights for you

We will respond as soon as possible and within the time required by law.
If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the
Data Protection Board of India.

## Children

KalpaXi is intended for adult users. If you are under 18, please register with the
consent of a parent or guardian.

## Security

The registration data is stored on a protected server, accessible only to the
KalpaXi maintainers. Installation codes are stored only in a secured (hashed)
form and expire after 5 minutes. If a data breach occurs, we will inform affected
users and the authorities as required by law.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
