# Validation: plate with a central hole

## Problem

Plate in tension with a central circular hole.

**Hand calculation**
- For a wide plate, the peak stress at the hole edge ≈ 3 × the remote stress (Kt ≈ 3).
- For a finite-width plate, use a published Kt chart (e.g. Peterson's) for d/W, with stress based on the net section.

**Suggested values:** W = 200 mm, d = 20 mm, t = 5 mm, remote stress 100 MPa.
